Capstone Project (Big Greasy)
Unity · C# | XR/VR
List of Contributions:
Level Design
Big Greasy was my VR capstone, a team-built game made in Unity. On the level design side, my job was shaping the space the player actually moves through and the things they interact with:
  • Exploring player interactions with the world and creating engaging level layouts.
  • Implementing NPCs with text and voice outputs (including recording the voice lines) for the player to interact with.
  • Making sure the level design supports the game's narrative and mechanics, including random objects the player can pick up and throw.
UI Developer
I also owned the game's UI, everything the player reads and clicks, with a focus on keeping it responsive in VR:
  • Creating interactions between the player and the world using an event-driven interface that avoids constant polling in the Update function.
  • Developing dynamic UI elements that provide information to the player about interactable objects.
  • Building the Rat Race UI, which was the highlight of this project. Coordinating between a UI Manager, the RaceTrack script (which determines race start and winner), and the RatMenu script (which manages player money) took careful system design, and it was the most rewarding work I did here.
In-Game HUD and Order System
Beyond the Rat Race menu, I built the heads-up display the player actually reads while working the shift. This shot catches it mid-haunting, with one of the game's ice events creeping in over the restaurant.
  • A persistent shift readout in the corner showing the day of the week, the in-game clock, and your current money, so you always know how deep into the night you are and how close you are to making rent.
  • Order information split across two surfaces: the active order on the kitchen monitor and the ingredient breakdown off to the side, so the player can read what to build without pausing or opening a menu, which matters a lot in VR.
  • A Next/Previous carousel for cycling through selectable items, with the row of slots showing what is available at a glance instead of hiding it behind a submenu.
  • A fear meter pinned to the top of the screen that surfaces the player's state during the supernatural events, turning something that would otherwise be pure atmosphere into feedback the player can actually read and react to.
